Frequently Asked Questions

How can I save my customized itinerary budgets? +

Your budgets save automatically right onto your computer or phone whenever you click the "Save" button. This means your plans will be waiting for you whenever you return to this website on the same device, even if you close the window!

Does TravelNest support offline capabilities? +

Mostly! You can still use the Budget Planner, the Random Trip Generator, and look at all your saved trips without internet. However, because the destination pictures are loaded from the web, you will need an active internet connection for the images to display properly.

How are matching prices generated? +

All lodging, daily food arrays, and attraction cost matrix tables populate natively from our optimized offline destinations database data file.
